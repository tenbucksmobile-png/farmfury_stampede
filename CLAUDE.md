# Farm Fury: Stampede

Side-scrolling platformer in the Farm Fury universe. Players pick one of eight farm animals and fight through hand-designed levels across six territories the Harvest Robots have occupied — running, jumping, and using each character's signature ability to defeat robots and reach the end of the level. Every level is completable by any unlocked character, but each also hides secrets that only one specific character's ability can reach. **Since 2026-09-28 the player can swap character at any time mid-level** (HUD swap button / Tab), and a level starts as whoever the player last played; there is no character pick before a level any more (this replaced the original character-before-level rule).

Full design spec: `FarmFury_Stampede_GDD.md`

Phase 5a architecture:
- **Robots** (`Scripts/Robots`): `RobotController` base (a contact is a stomp/ability hit via overridable `TakeHit()`; bosses override it and `CanHurtPlayer`). `GroundPatrolRobot` holds the patrol/wall/ledge logic; `HarvesterRobot` is it as-is, `ScoutRobot` (speed 3.8; turns to face a player who is behind it within sight range with line of sight), `ChaserRobot` (fixed 6.4 u/s vs the player's 8; wakes inside its aggro range = marker patrolDistance; stops at walls/ledges; stompable). `BarrierUnit` (1x3 solid on the Ground layer, refuses every hit, only Billy's Charge Break via `IChargeBreakable`; on open ground it can be jumped over, so use it only in capped passages such as a Chamber). `RobotType.Commander` was appended to the enum.
- **Boss pattern** (`CommanderBoss`, read the comment block at the top): boss level = normal level prefab with `Boss()` (no goal); 3 hits, 1.5s stagger between hits (harmless, un-hittable), speeds up per hit, reinforcement waves via `RobotSpawnPoint.wave` spawned by `LevelLoader.SpawnWave(n)` on the nth hit; defeat -> `GameManager.BossDefeated` (since 2026-10-01: every robot falls via `LevelLoader.DefeatAllRobots`, 7 bursts over the boss in 0.4x slow motion (since 2026-10-03 the `Sprites/UI/ImpactStars.png` stars via `AbilityPrefabs.victoryEffect` / `VictoryEffect.prefab`, built by setup; was Bessie's BessieSlam art), a 1.2s beat; no death/pause/swap/Locker meanwhile via `BossVictoryPending`) -> `CompleteLevel()`; Level Complete then opens under `WorldClearedScreen`: since 2026-10-03 only the World Unlocked page (the next world's card revealed from a silhouette, rays, confetti, "Next stop"), and only when the clear opened a world; the WORLD CLEARED! page was removed. Meadow Ruins' boss level was lengthened 2026-10-01: a 110-unit approach (terraces to top 4, four gaps, hay stack, Chaser, 4 checkpoints) before the same arena at [110,170). Compiled and validated headless, not yet seen in Play mode. Worlds 2-6 duplicate the prefab/RobotData and author a new arena.
- **Double jump**: every character gets `airJumps` (1) extra mid-air jumps at full jump velocity (variable height, reset on landing; `CharacterController2D`). `LevelBuilder`'s reach validation still models only the single base jump, so it can't catch a secret that a double jump now reaches - Level 1's Cluck ledge was lowered to 4 units so the double jump reaches it comfortably (a single jump doesn't).
- **Camera** (`CameraFollow2D`, base orthographic size 7.5 set by setup): X follow with look-ahead; Y dead zone + offset, framed against the level *ground* below the player - the nearest `Ground` / `BreakableFloor` tilemap surface, looking through the `Platforms` tilemap (ledges, floating platforms, stone blocks) and obstacle colliders - which stays `floorMargin` (2) above the bottom edge with `topMargin` (2.5) of headroom above the player. When both don't fit (the player high on a ledge) it zooms out smoothly, up to `maxOrthographicSize` (11), and back in afterwards (2026-09-27, was: the first surface under the player, so the ground vanished when standing on a ledge; not yet playtested). Over a pit the last ground seen is kept; falling below it the camera just follows. Underground or secret sections below the surface frame their own floor the same way. `ParallaxLayer` widths are sized for the max zoom. **HUD reserve** (2026-09-29, not yet seen in Play mode): the HUD writes the screen fraction its bottom control row covers to `CameraFollow2D.BottomScreenReserve` every frame (from the jump button's screen corners), and the floor is kept that high plus `reserveGap` (0.3) instead of `floorMargin` when larger, so the buttons sit over the dirt, not the path; the zoom-out maths accounts for it. Build any new underground/secret floor on the Ground tilemap (not Platforms) so the camera treats it as floor.
- **Lives**: 3 per attempt (`GameManager.LivesPerAttempt`). Every death (pit, robot, drowning) costs one and respawns at the checkpoint; the third calls `EndLevel(false)` -> the Level Failed screen, which waits for a button (retry / Level Select / home). `StartLevel` always resets to 3.
- **Progression rules** (`SaveManager`): a world unlocks when the previous world's boss level is cleared (`SetWorldBossCleared` is the test hook); a level unlocks when the previous level in its world is completed; the boss unlocks after all regular levels. Stars (`StarCalculator`): 1 completed, +1 for >=75% of normal crops, +1 for finding the level's gated secret (or finishing without dying if it has none); time is not scored yet. "Secret found" = a secret-cluster crop was collected (`CropPickup.isSecretCluster`, persisted), which drives the Level Select "?" icon.

## Key architectural facts (from the GDD)

- **8 characters**: Cluck (Egg Launch - lobs an egg that defeats a robot; was Flutter Jump, changed 2026-09-26, starter), Bessie (Ground Pound, starter; slowed 2026-10-01: dive 24 u/s (was 30), quake 11 u/s (was 16); since 2026-09-30 it also works on the ground as a foot stamp, and every slam sends a quake along the ground the way she faces - small `BessieSlam.png` bursts via `FadeEffect.Play(size)`, 16 u/s for 10 u, stops at a wall/pit, a full-size burst defeats the first robot it reaches; constants in `GroundPoundAbility`; not yet compiled or playtested), Percy (Roll Dash, unlock @5 levels), Woolly (Cloud Step, @10), Ducky (Skip Dash, @15), Horace (Horseshoe Throw - lobs `horseshoe.png` in the same arc as Cluck's egg (slowed 2026-10-01: forward 10, up 4.5, gravity 17, spin 540, lifetime 2 - was 13/5/22/720/1.6), on the ground or in the air; was Rear Vault on the ground + a straight throw in the air, changed 2026-09-30, not yet compiled or playtested; `AbilityType.RearVaultThrow` keeps its name, @20), Gerald (Puff Glide, @30), Billy (Charge Break, @40).
- **Design rule**: every main path through every level must be completable by *any* unlocked character — abilities gate secrets and bonus areas, never the critical path.
- **5 robot types**: Harvester (ground patrol), Scout (fast patrol), Drone (flying, needs a jump-into or Horace's horseshoe), Barrier Unit (stationary, only Billy's Charge Break clears it), Chaser (pursues at a fixed sub-player speed).
- **6 worlds**, reused from the shelved original Farm Fury's world designs, reframed as platformer territories: Meadow Ruins (tutorial), Frozen Tundra (reduced ice traction), Watermill Village (fire-spread hazards), Sky Islands (wind gusts), Sunken City (reduced-gravity underwater), Robot Mothership (zero-G, Robot Overlord finale). 12 levels per world (11 + the boss as the 12th), 72 total for v1 launch (raised from 46 in GDD v1.2).
- **Controller genuinely differs from Rush's** despite the shared engine: Rush keeps the character fixed in X while the world scrolls past it; Stampede needs a conventional platformer setup where the *character* traverses a Tilemap-space level with a following camera. The Physics2D lesson from Rush still applies — anything needing reliable collision must use a kinematic `Rigidbody2D` moved via `MovePosition`, never `transform.Translate`.
- **Level authoring**: Unity Tilemap + Composite Collider2D for geometry, with level content still defined as data (ScriptableObject descriptors referencing tilemap layouts), matching the franchise's data-driven convention.
- **Ability model** (since 2026-09-29, Arcade's setup; was a 3-uses-per-level limit): a 5 s cooldown after every use (`CharacterData.abilityCooldown`, `CharacterController2D.CooldownRemaining`/`SkipCooldown`, scaled time so it waits while paused; remembered per character across swaps, reset when a level starts). The HUD greys the ability card (no countdown text since 2026-10-03); a spinning coin badge (68 px with the smaller buttons, was 84/64; no price text since 2026-10-03) on its top-left corner skips it for `HudScreen.SkipCooldownCoinsCost` (3, Arcade's) coins (greyed when unaffordable) and a Watch Ad button above the card skips it for a rewarded ad (`skip_cooldown_via_ad`, shown only while an ad is loaded, level frozen meanwhile). Headless-tested (use -> 5 s countdown, press while cooling ignored, coin skip spends 3, swap keeps the cooldown, natural recharge); the ad path is untested (no ad key) and none of it is seen in Play mode yet.
